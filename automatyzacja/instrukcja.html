NWAR quasi-2D
Running and extending waves

Open START_NWAR.vbs in the nwar_automation folder. Closing the browser tab does not stop calculations. Keep the computer on and awake, with sufficient disk space and an available STAR-CCM+ licence.

One more cycle

  1. Select a wave.
  2. Choose Extra cycles from wave checkpoint and 1 cycle.
  3. Click Run selected. The controller selects the latest verified healthy checkpoint, excluding technical tests.
  4. Follow physical time, CD, CL, CFL, step cost and estimated time remaining. The report updates automatically after saving.

An extra cycle is scheduled using the encounter period. Acceptance uses complete cycles between measured probe crossings. Wait for an external continuation to finish saving before requesting its next segment.

Full history and resume markers

The live chart loads the complete CL/CD history and all continuation segments. Separate vertical scales share the physical-time axis. Dashed vertical lines identify each resume time. Hover or tap for sample values. The latest saved history remains visible after completion.

The browser preserves local extrema when reducing display points; CSV and MAT exports contain all samples. Missing sections remain gaps. Full-history report figures and MATLAB plots include the same resume markers.

New wave from C01

Select waves, choose New run from C01 and start. Runs use one GPU in sequence and finish after four complete repeatable cycles. Diagnostic limits are 20 cycles or 12 hours. W04, W06 and W07 remain unavailable pending the reviews shown on their cards.

Pause and resume

Pause queue and save requests a checkpoint after the current step. Wait for Pause saved. Resume checkpoint starts a new segment with existing cycle history and remaining budget. Continue queue releases waiting jobs without resuming saved pauses automatically. Cancel applies to queued jobs. Controls do not stop runs owned by other controllers.

After a crash or restart, interrupted jobs require checkpoint review. Solver failure, inconsistent saves or diagnostic limits pause the queue for inspection.

Reports and exports

Open report shows the latest report. Download full package downloads a standalone ZIP. Extract it and open report.html without the dashboard or internet. Rebuild report refreshes saved results without starting a solver.

Acceptance and Q

Accepted · qualitative stage requires accepted C01 lineage, preserved configuration, a healthy saved result and five complete measured cycles with repeatable cycles 2-5; cycle 1 is excluded. The mesh review was completed before C01. Expand Run validation to inspect 12 checks and their evidence. Future studies are separately collapsed and do not block this stage.

Q = mean wave CD − mean C01 CD. Negative values indicate drag reduction. ΔD = Q × 3855.6 N, with percentage change also shown. C01 is approximately 27.00 N. Wave means use complete measured cycles 2-5, excluding cycle 1. Click Update plot to collect newly saved accepted results; updates also run automatically.

Force–wave plots

Each saved run receives a force–wave plot when complete measured cycles are available. It shows up to four individual cycles, their mean response and force–wave loops. Force curves show L − L_C01 and D − D_C01 in newtons: zero is the fixed original C01, and wave-cycle mean offsets remain visible. All wave phases refer to x = 0. Older wave signals are propagated from upstream probes; new runs record x = 0 directly. Partial runs are labelled. Arrows indicate time direction. The combined four-panel figure adds each accepted wave automatically and includes PDF, SVG, 600 dpi PNG and CSV downloads. Live histories show CD − CD_C01 and CL − CL_C01; pointing at the curve also displays the original coefficients.
